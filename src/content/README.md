## Deliberately omitted (could not be verified)

These are intentionally NOT on the site. Supply official figures and they can
be added to the matching content module:

- Total student enrolment, alumni count
- Placement percentage, highest/average package, recruiter count
- Number of schools/faculties, research labs and innovation centres
- NAAC grade and NIRF rank
- Count of international collaborations / MoUs beyond the SJHIFM MoU
- Personal awards conferred on Mr. Ankush Bansal as an individual
- Verbatim press-attributed quotations from Mr. Bansal
- Student testimonials
- Official YouTube videos of his speeches

Self-reported LinkedIn figures (e.g. "17+ years of experience") are labelled
as such in the UI rather than presented as independently verified.
